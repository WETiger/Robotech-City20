RC 366

Smaller, Faster, Stronger

Overview of RC 366

The RC-366 stands out as the epitome of a collaborative, industrial robot, thanks to its compact size. Tailored for optimal performance in restricted workspaces, it delivers unparalleled flexibility and accuracy. Whether placed on a tabletop alongside employees or integrated into a dedicated workstation for tasks like picking, assembling, and positioning parts, this cobot excels.

Its diminutive footprint and lightweight construction enable seamless integration into any production setting, augmenting human labor and boosting productivity. Equipped with state-of-the-art safety features, it ensures secure cooperation between humans and machines.

Upgrade your operations, elevate quality standards, and maintain a competitive edge with this compact automation solution.
